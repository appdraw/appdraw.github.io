Frequently asked questions

How do I turn my drawing into an animation?

Draw anything on the canvas, tap Animate, then pick a picture or type text with the Aa tile. The app will turn your drawing into it.

Where are my animations saved?

Tap Save on the result screen to save a GIF or MP4 to your gallery, or Share to send it to other apps. If saving fails, allow photo access for AppDraw in your device settings.

Can I use a photo as a background?

Yes. Tap the background button on the drawing screen and pick a photo. The photo stays on your device.

How do I change ad or tracking settings?

iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can also change your ad consent in the app menu > Privacy settings.

How do I request deletion of my data?

Email support@appdraw.app. See section 7 of our Privacy Policy.
