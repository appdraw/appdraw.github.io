How it works

No drawing skills needed. Free, no sign-up.

✏️

Draw or type

Doodle with brushes and colors, add stickers, or type a word or an emoji. Pick a photo as the background if you like.

✨

Pick what it becomes

Choose from 6,000+ pictures and 15 motion styles: Bounce, Jelly, Spring and more. Play it forward, in reverse or in a loop.

📤

Save and share

Export a GIF or MP4 to your gallery or send it straight to any messenger. Great for greetings, love notes and memes.
